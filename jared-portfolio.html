Operations · Onboarding

Tech onboarding: one form sets up every new real estate agent

Problem
Every new real estate agent who joined the brokerage had to be set up by hand across several systems, with dozens of fields and permissions to get right each time.
Built
One form that creates the real estate agent's GoHighLevel user with the right fields and permissions, links their records across our CRMs, and resizes and uploads their headshot. If something is missing, it emails the right operations person to finish it. Every user it creates is logged.
Result
Real estate agent setup went from a checklist to a single form, done the same way every time, with gaps flagged automatically.

Reporting · Dashboard

Lead-to-close reporting with agent KPIs

Problem
Partner lead data was split between two CRMs, so nobody could see where leads were stalling.
Built
A live dashboard that pulls from GoHighLevel and Follow Up Boss and tracks every lead from beginning to end: agent attached, appointment set, met, listed, under contract, and closed. It breaks the numbers down by agent, so each agent's KPIs are visible.
Result
Leadership can see exactly who is converting and who isn't, then coach, retrain, or remove agents as the numbers dictate. Appointments with no update are flagged for follow-up.

Operations · Lead processing

Partner lead import: one CSV, three systems

Problem
A referral partner sends client lists as spreadsheets. Every lead has to land in three systems, with the right agent and the right lead source, before anyone can work it.
Built
Upload the CSV once. The app matches each lead to an editable 48-agent roster, decides the lead source, and sends it by webhook into GoHighLevel, Follow Up Boss, and RMS, our internal Referral Management System. Sends are rate limited so no lead gets dropped.
Result
One upload does the work of three separate imports, with the routing decisions already made.

Music · Phone app

Hidden Gold: Spotify couldn't do it, so I built it

Problem
I wanted to hear the songs I've never played from bands I already love. Spotify has no way to find them, and its recommendations keep serving the same hits.
Built
My own app on top of Spotify. It reads my listening history, finds every song I've played two times or fewer, and serves them to a playlist. Each band shows how many songs are left to dig, and it tracks a hit rate for the songs I end up saving.
Result
A playlist of new music I'm likely to love, with the stats to prove it.
