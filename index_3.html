Work · GoHighLevel

Nationwide lead routing with custom geocoding

Partner leads were arriving from more than 30 states with an address and nothing else: no agent, no territory, no appointment.

I built a custom code step inside a GoHighLevel workflow that geocodes each address, matches it to one of 152 agent territories (smallest boundary wins when they overlap), and sets the lead's local timezone with daylight saving handled, so the booking bot offers real appointment times. The lead then flows into Follow Up Boss with the right agent attached, and the consultation is booked through the FUB API.

Result: every lead gets the right agent and a correctly timed appointment, with no manual sorting.

Work · AI automation

AI triage for replies to automated email

Our CRM sends automated email all day, and agents reply to it with real questions. Those replies landed in one shared inbox where they waited for someone to read and forward them.

I built an automation that reads every reply, classifies it with AI, and routes it to the right team: Tech, Processing, Mission Control, Marketing, Onboarding, or Legal. Anything tied to a legal matter goes straight to our legal team. An earlier version I built in Google Apps Script answered with a link to a support ticket app I also built.

Result: fewer support tickets and faster answers for agents.

Personal project

Hidden Gold: finding songs I've never heard

Streaming apps keep playing the same hits. I wanted the deep cuts from bands I already love.

Hidden Gold reads my listening history, finds each band's songs I've played fewer than three times, and builds a Spotify playlist out of them. Each band shows how many songs are left to dig, and once I've heard them all it's marked done.

Result: a playlist that's always new music I'm likely to love.
